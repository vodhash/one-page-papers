On this site

Every poster has its page here, with its PDFs in each format and theme, its full text to read on screen, set from the same source as the poster, and a wallpaper for a phone, a desktop or a 4K screen, which your browser draws from the PDF.

The repository

The engine, the texts and the record of their sources and licenses are on GitHub: github.com/vodhash/one-page-papers. Each poster is built from its text and a meta.yaml file, which gives its source, its edition and its license.

A text is missing? Suggest it and vote for the next ones in Discussions, or request a poster, with its source and what you know of its license; the contributing guide gives the checklists that every text goes through.
