<p>We are uncovering better ways of developing<br>software by doing it and helping others do it.<br>Through this work we have come to value:</p>

<div class="values"><p><span class="l">Individuals and interactions</span> over processes and tools</p><p><span class="l">Working software</span> over comprehensive documentation</p><p><span class="l">Customer collaboration</span> over contract negotiation</p><p><span class="l">Responding to change</span> over following a plan</p></div>

<p>That is, while there is value in the items on<br>the right, we value the items on the left more.</p>
